    KPIDefinition

    ProfitSum of order item profit, same exclusions
    Profit margin %Profit ÷ revenue

    Data notes

    • Source. DataCo Smart Supply Chain (Constante, Silva & Pereira, 2019), Mendeley Data v5, DOI 10.17632/8gx2fvg2k6.5, CC BY 4.0. Currency is treated as USD.
    • Excluded statuses. CANCELED and SUSPECTED_FRAUD items (7,754) are excluded from every KPI and from this page's data.
    • Default range. Jan 2015 to Sep 2017. From Oct 2017 every order has one item, the catalogue changes and almost all buyers are new, so trends and cohorts stop there. Extend "To" to see the full range.
    • Market rotates with time. In a typical month most items go to one market, so market comparisons are also period comparisons.
    • Discount rate. The rate actually applied (discount ÷ sales). Rows listed at 6% were applied at 5.5%.
    • Observational. Every comparison is an association, not a cause. The what-if states its assumptions on the page.
